Terms/Vocabulary
CHEM 121
Science uses highly precise language. As a result, students in a first-year science course are often
introduced to as many—or even more—new vocabulary terms as students in a first-year foreign language
course. In addition, science students must learn both the terminology and the underlying concepts at the
same time, whereas foreign language students are often learning new words for ideas and objects they
already understand.
To help make this process easier, we have compiled a list of key terms for each topic, along with the
concepts associated with them. The terms are organized by topic, and we recommend reviewing them
before we begin each unit so that you can recognize and focus on them during lectures.
After each lecture, take time to review the terms and write your own definitions. Do not wait until just
before an exam. Once a term has been introduced, it will be used regularly in class, so it is important that
you understand and can use the vocabulary by the next lecture.
