the winter (after all of the leaves have dropped)? Give your answer in pounds. Note: 1.00 pound
= 453.6 g.
12. In 2016, Morgan Korn from ABC News reported that the average American eats 3,000 Calories

mile. If you walked 3.5 miles per hour, how many hours would you have to walk to burn the 3.00
x 103 Calories from Thanksgiving dinner?

gallon = 3.78 L.

19. Determine the concentration of each of the following.
a. When we make solutions, we first dissolve the solute in a small amount of solvent. Then, we
add solvent to the final volume. Why do we do it this way rather than simply adding the
solute to the final volume of solvent? Why do we make sure to dissolve the solution before
adding the final volume of solvent instead of just adding solvent while there is still
undissolved solute?
b. A salt solution is made by dissolving 55.0 g of NaCl in 50.0 mL of water and then the volume
is brought up to 75.0 mL with additional water. What is the concentration in g/mL? What is
the concentration in mg/mL? What is the concentration in g/L?
c. A glucose solution is made by dissolving 0.500 mole of glucose in 100 mL of water and then
the volume is brought up to 1.65 liter with additional water. What is the concentration in
molarity (M)? What is the concentration in millimolarity (mM)? What is the concentration in
micromolarity (M)?
d. An aspirin solution is made by dissolving 0.0050 mmole of aspirin in 100 L of ethanol and
then the volume is brought up to 10.0 mL with additional ethanol. What is the concentration
in molarity (M)? What is the concentration in millimolarity (mM)? What is the concentration
in micromolarity (M)?
