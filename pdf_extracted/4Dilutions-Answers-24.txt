1. We make stock solutions & then dilute for our desired concentration for two main reasons: 1) It tends
to be more time consuming to weigh out the mass of a solid and dissolve it each time we make a
solution. It is faster to simply measure a volume of a stock solution. 2) We often have to make a
solution with so little of the solid that it can be difficult to get an accurate mass (e.g., it is easier to
measure 1.0 g than 0.100 mg). We therefore make a concentrated stock solution and dilute to get our
desired concentration.
