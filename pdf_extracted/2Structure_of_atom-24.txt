STRUCTURE OF THE ATOM STUDY GUIDE
Chemistry 121
Problems:
1. Define the following subatomic particles and note their location in an atom:
a. Electron
b. Proton
c. Neutron
2. What is the difference between an orbital and an orbit? What is the maximum number of electrons that
can occupy one orbital? What is the minimum?
3. Are electrons massless? Why do we treat them as if they have no mass when determining the mass of
an isotope?
4. What is an isotope? How is it possible to have atoms of the same element but different masses?
5. Do the masses on the periodic table correlate to a specific isotope? How are the masses on the periodic
table calculated?
6. If intelligent life is found on another planet, would you expect their periodic table to have the same
masses noted for the elements as our periodic table? Explain.
